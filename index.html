One Swipe Switch

A logic puzzle game for iPhone.

The game

One swipe moves every dot on a 5×5 board one step in the same direction. Walls and edges block the way and dots never overlap. Guide each dot to its own goal at the same time, in as few moves as you can. 1000 campaign levels, a Daily Challenge, and play offline without an account.
